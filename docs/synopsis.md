# Synopsis - Shin Masou Kishin: Panzer Warfare

Reference for translators. Story order = event file order (35 files, one per chapter). Save-slot labels
(script/overlay.json, "...まで終了") name the chapters; English titles below are working glosses, not binding.
SPOILERS: later sections and lines marked REVEAL. Don't let a line hint at a reveal before its file.

No plot branches: the 35 labels are linear, and the only `choice` menus are the ev000 tutorial menus
(the other `choice`-kind lines are ordinary boxes). Every chapter ends with a battle; the stock defeat/retreat
lines (くっそぉっ！オレは、こんなところで…… etc.) repeat in every file.

Scroll pages: each file from ev010 on holds two chapter-intro scroll pages, written as solemn, poetic narration.
They look back on what just happened and set up the chapter (e.g. the ev03E pages mourn the assassination from ev033).

## World
- A-Zeluth: world powered by spirits (精霊) and Prana (a person's life energy). Monsters pour out of Daus, the
  Abyssal Pit; under it lies a dark "underworld" (also called Daus) where time runs differently (days there =
  half a year on the surface).
- Three kingdoms era: Vartzpheem (holy kingdom, centre of alchemy and magic, heroes' homeland), Ragash
  (militarist, rearming), Natoure (neutral monarchy).
- Masouki: humanoid war machines modelled on the Arm of God, a relic machine found 100 years ago.
  Masoukishin: the four elemental god-machines (Cybuster wind, Zamzeed earth, Granveil fire, Gaddess water);
  a pilot must form a contract (契約) with the machine's spirit, and the spirit picks the pilot.
- Knight name titles (the middle name is a rank, not a family name): Zants = junior/apprentice knight (Keigo before
  his knighting, the trio); Kurtz = full knight, granted at the knighting (ev010); Gram = royal blood (Alma
  Gram Karina, Rieg Gram Garum); Rasm = "god" title (Alfredo Rasm Yuro, from Rasm Iknaat written on the Arm of
  God); Qual (Toto). So "Keigo Zants Ferdinand" in ev000/ev010 is correct, not a typo.
- Rieg Gram Garum: legendary hero of 100 years ago, said to have founded the Masou knights and died young in an
  accident. His motto: "Let there be light upon them, the brave knights!" (彼ら、勇敢なる騎士たちに光あれ！).

## Cast
Western Knights (西方騎士隊), Vartzpheem's elite Masouki unit:
- Keigo Kurtz Ferdinand: protagonist, youngest cadet ever to pass the knight exam; reckless, earnest, hot-headed.
  Later pilot of Cybuster. Falls for Alma.
- Alma Kurtz Karina, really Alma Gram Karina: captain, princess of Vartzpheem (hides it). Proud, capable, guilt-
  ridden about Mira. Pilots Tilwes, later Gaddess.
- Beumer Kurtz Baum: vice-captain, joking big-brother type, flirts with Shess; leads the Liberation Army, later
  captain of the new Western Knights.
- Furcas Kurtz Cambie: veteran knight, Zeldna's senior, teacher of "how to survive"; pilots Smaug.
- Helena Zants Sugar, Blacky Zants Katis, Drawbi Zants Raymon: the comic trio ("3人組"). Helena = brash
  big-sister; Blacky and Drawbi call her "Helena-sama".
- Shess: unit signal operator (calls in enemy contacts), sweet on Keigo.
Vartzpheem:
- Burgess: King of Vartzpheem, father of Alma and Mira. Knights Keigo in ev010.
- Ruggero: royal priest/court official who conducts the knighting. Traitor (REVEAL ev090).
- Rossellini: High Priest (神官長), kindly mentor of Beumer and co.; later voice of the Liberation Army.
- Zeldna, "Zeldna the Flash Blade": Keigo's instructor, once the strongest knight. Defects (REVEAL ev0CA).
- Alfredo Rasm Yuro: Chief Engineer (技師長), master of the Iknaat Mansion, creator of the Masouki and the
  Masoukishin. Lecherous, boastful old man; calls Alma "Alma-chan".
- Toto Qual Yuro: Alfredo's grandson, a boy lost in Daus; pilots Zamzeed.
Natoure:
- Shana: Princess of Natoure; gentle, wise, worried by an evil she senses.
- Yen Yang: Natoure holy knight (聖騎士), pilot of Granveil; devoted to Alma, cool to Keigo, slowly warms up.
- Jems: Shana's old retainer (harrumphs).
Ragash:
- Duncan: King of Ragash, wants peace; assassinated in ev033.
- Adenoid Sellers: Ragash's Supreme Commander (総帥), schemer who seizes power after Duncan; pilots Ag Gebra.
- Thodd Justo: Adenoid's cold, mysterious strategist; builds Majuuki (魔重機) machines. Really Rieg Gram Garum
  (REVEAL ev179; hinted at in ev0A1, ev124, ev16B).
- Mira Karina: Alma's younger sister, pilot of a white Masouki, fights for Ragash out of hatred for Alma.
- Yadora: crude Ragash pilot who loves fighting, calls the heroes "kids" (坊や).
- Magia Magus: Ragash knight, stoic, lives only for battle; Kyari's partner.
- Kyari: Ragash woman pilot, brash and sardonic; partner of Magia.
- Malcolm: the new (child) King of Ragash at the treaty (ev179); Dison: a Ragash official there.
- Luca Silence: (dead before the story) the strongest knight, Mira's fiance, killed by Alma in a duel.

## Chapters
1. ev000 "Masou Knight": Keigo dozes through Zeldna's last lecture, then takes the final knight exam (tutorial
   vs. Golems). Monsters appear; Captain Alma saves the cadets and lectures Keigo, who resents her. Thodd and
   Ruggero plot: Vartzpheem "betrayed by its own priest"; Thodd wants the Arm of God.
2. ev010 "The Arm of God": King Burgess knights Keigo (Kurtz). Zeldna sends him to the Iknaat Mansion, where
   Alfredo explains the Arm of God and his machines Jameim/Tilwes. Keigo fights monsters in Jameim unordered;
   Alma and Beumer arrive and draft him into the Western Knights.
3. ev01C "Premonition": introductions at the base; mission to watch Daus, where non-monster Prana is detected.
   Mira and Yadora watch the unit. Thodd tells Adenoid the people need a "just cause".
4. ev027 "Those Who Face Each Other": Alfredo shows Keigo a Masouki's insides. Yadora and Mira stage a border
   incident: Ragash claims the Western Knights invaded.
5. ev033 "The Rolling Stone": Ruggero sides with Ragash's version at court; Burgess asks Alma to quit knighthood
   and come home, mentions Mira missing. King Duncan visits for peace talks; an unknown Masouki kills him in front
   of the knights; Yadora frames Vartzpheem, and Adenoid declares war.
6. ev03E "Fate": Keigo hates fighting people. Mira, in a white Masouki, attacks Alma, who freezes. REVEAL: Mira
   is Alma's sister, out for revenge ("you took what I loved most"). Keigo snaps Alma out of it.
7. ev049 "Duel! The Western Knights": friendly tournament (Keigo vs. the trio, Furcas, Beumer; both win and
   loss lines exist, picked by the battle result), drinking party. Alfredo's family vanished exploring Daus (another world).
8. ev05B "Prelude to Collapse": Rossellini comforts Alma. Ruggero strips Ardennes Island of troops and leaves only
   the Western Knights; Ragash invades in force, Mira and Yadora attack; Thodd demands surrender.
9. ev065 "Recklessness and Courage": the unit must hold the rearguard; Keigo recalls "recklessness is not
   courage". Yen arrives in the Masoukishin Granveil and saves them.
10. ev06E "Road to the Capital": the kingdom is overrun. REVEAL: Beumer tells Keigo Alma is Alma Gram Karina, the
    king's daughter, a candidate Masoukishin pilot. The capital is under siege; Yen clashes with Keigo.
11. ev078 "Masoukishin": Burgess sends Alma to Iknaat to contract the finished Masoukishin Cybuster. Zeldna is
    missing in action. Cybuster rejects Alma. Yadora attacks (a traitor told Ragash where Iknaat is); Keigo calls
    on Cybuster and it awakens for him (REVEAL: Keigo is the contractor), then warps him and Alma away.
12. ev086 "Alma": Keigo dreams of Zeldna's lessons ("be ruthless once battle starts"). In a dark world, Alma is
    bitter that Cybuster chose him; Keigo swears loyalty ("I'll stay by your side"). Alma: "sounds like you're
    wooing a lover."
13. ev090 "Yin and Yang": days in the underworld; they meet hungry Toto, Alfredo's grandson, pilot of Zamzeed
    (Alfredo built it in secret). REVEAL: Ruggero betrays Burgess; the capital falls; Rossellini stays behind,
    Beumer breaks out with the survivors.
14. ev09B "Forest of Evil Spirits": Toto leads them toward the Daus hole through dimensional currents. Adenoid
    and Thodd plan to take Granveil and the last Masoukishin.
15. ev0A1 "Cry of the Forsaken": they find a corpse and a diary full of hatred, signed Rieg Gram Garum
    (foreshadowing: the hero did not die in an accident). Spirits attack Alma's mind about Mira. Alma tells Keigo
    her past: she killed Luca Silence, Mira's fiance, by accident in the final of the pilot-candidate tournament;
    Mira vanished; Alma joined the Western Knights to replace him. Keigo: "I'll be your sword."
16. ev0AF "Daus": Yen reports to Shana in Natoure. In Daus, endless monsters; Alma starts falling for Keigo.
    Alfredo and Shess are safe in Natoure.
17. ev0B8 "The Crossing Point": Thodd tests his new machine in Daus, meets the three, reveals Ruggero as his
    informant and the capital's fall, demands Cybuster; Kyari appears. A dimensional current swallows them.
18. ev0C2 "The Path": a silent voice talks with Keigo in his thoughts; they are back on the surface. Kyari
    attacks for sport; Yen arrives and escorts them toward Natoure. Ruggero orders an interception.
19. ev0CA "Taste of Betrayal": half a year has passed. REVEAL: Zeldna, now with Ragash, blocks their way ("you
    are my enemy"); Yadora mocks the reunion. Thodd gives Magia and Kyari his Majuuki.
20. ev0D6 "A New Battle": Shana asks the Masoukishin pilots to help stop Ragash. Alma learns her father was
    killed. Alma contracts the Masoukishin Gaddess, given by Shana. Kyari and Magia attack.
21. ev0E4 "Hidden Power": Alfredo reunites with Toto, installs the Akashic Drive; he suspects Zeldna had a reason.
    Shess arrives: Beumer leads the Vartzpheem Liberation Army. Alma tells Yen she wants strength to protect Keigo.
22. ev0F1 "Parting": Yen leaves Natoure with them. Ruggero, now with Thodd, plans to use Mira and Zeldna. Alma
    defeats Mira and spares her. Zeldna duels Keigo: he sided with Ragash for money to treat his sick daughter;
    she died anyway; he dies proud of Keigo. Yadora ambushes and burns to death.
23. ev103 "Those Who Tread on the Tiger's Tail": Furcas defends the Liberation Army's village; the four
    Masoukishin save him. Adenoid begins to distrust Thodd.
24. ev10A "Seven Knights of the Wasteland": reunion with Beumer; Magia and Kyari raid the base and are trapped.
    Helena, Blacky and Drawbi return with news: Rossellini is held at the Altadomus Temple.
25. ev119 "Liberation": the rebels rescue Rossellini from Magia's assault. Yen on how a "tide" turns.
26. ev124 "カミチル闇" (title unclear): Thodd works at Iknaat on the Arm of God, which resonates with Cybuster
    (same wind spirit: Cybuster's spirit was forced into it). Thodd escapes with it.
27. ev131 "Not as a God but as a Man": Rossellini tells Shana he will leave how the Masoukishin's power is used to the
   young pilots' conscience, not to nations. Mira guards a Prana
    Bomb that would also kill Ragash troops; Keigo appeals to her; she helps defuse it and asks him to look after
    "big sister".
28. ev13D "Shadows Moving in Secret": Shess and Toto tease Keigo about Alma. Ruggero's surprise raid fails;
    Adenoid orders him killed if he fails; Ruggero dies cursing an unfair world.
29. ev14B "Gunfire": Rossellini's speech; march on the capital; Magia given full command. Kyari holds the line
    and dies in Magia's arms ("we still have to go drinking together").
30. ev15A "When Ambition Ends (Part 1)": Magia refuses to surrender, trades names with Keigo. Adenoid readies Ag
    Gebra.
31. ev162 "When Ambition Ends (Part 2)": Adenoid's Ag Gebra; he preaches a world for the able; he dies raging
    "why does no one accept me". Beumer becomes captain of the new Western Knights. Victory.
32. ev16B "The Feast Begins": Vartzpheem becomes a republic; ceasefire. Magia fights a private last battle and
    dies. Thodd appears ("the feast has already begun"), is beaten and seems to die. Alma plans to travel once
    peace comes; Keigo asks to go with her.
33. ev179 "Advent of the Evil God": three-nation treaty with the child king Malcolm. REVEAL: Thodd returns,
    names himself Rieg Gram Garum, "king" wronged by the forebears and shut in the world's underside; Ragash
    vanishes into a rift in the sky.
34. ev185 "The Last Hope": Daus monsters swarm; Mira returns to help ("Big sister"). REVEAL (Rossellini to
    Shana): Garum, obsessed with alchemy and creating a "more perfect human", committed atrocities; the royals
    tried to kill him, he leapt into the Daus pit, and his end was covered up as an accident.
35. ev18D "To a Shining Tomorrow": Thodd's story: the rulers backed his human experiments, then tried to kill him;
    he survived alone in Daus, concluded perfect humans can't exist and became something beyond human. REVEAL: he
    killed King Burgess and threw him into Daus. Final battle vs. Izrafel, "the only true Masoukishin". Dying, Thodd
    feels sadness and fear and understands "that is human". Epilogue: monsters gone, Vartzpheem a republic, Beumer
    stays a knight (and jokes about marrying Shess); Keigo and Alma leave together on a journey.
