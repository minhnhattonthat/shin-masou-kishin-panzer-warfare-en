# Style reference - character voices

Read with docs/translator-brief.md (Style) and glossary.md (names, Style rules). Lines counted over all event files
(speaker labels; battle quotes not counted). JP markers are from the script itself. The aim: a reader can tell who
is speaking from the English alone.

General:
- Dialogue: no quote marks; thoughts （…） -> (parentheses); ……/… -> `...`; stammers keep their shape (I-I...).
- Honorifics only where the JP has them: アルマ様 Alma-sama (Yen, Rossellini, Ruggero), イェン殿 Yen-dono,
  ケイゴ殿 Keigo-dono (Shana, Rossellini), ケイゴさん Keigo-san (Shess), ケイゴはん Keigo-han (Drawbi, Kansai -san),
  ヘレーナ様 Helena-sama (Blacky, Drawbi), アルマちゃん Alma-chan (Alfredo). Titles become English: 隊長 Captain,
  副長 Vice-Captain, 技師長 Chief Engineer, 神官長 High Priest, 教官 Instructor, 陛下 Your Majesty, 王女 Princess.
- Family words used as names: にいちゃん "big bro", ねえちゃん "sis", じいちゃん "Grandpa", じいさん "old man"/"Gramps",
  おじさん "mister"/"uncle", 姉さん "big sister"/"sis".
- Stock lines recur in every chapter (defeat/retreat cries): translate them once and reuse the same English.
